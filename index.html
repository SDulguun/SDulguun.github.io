Loan Default Risk Analysis
Lending Club 2007–2018 · 2026
SQL + Tableau analysis of 2.3 million consumer loans.
Problem
Who should a lender lend to, and at what price? I used Lending Club's full 2007–2018 loan book — every loan they funded, with both the borrower's profile and whether they paid it back — to find out.
Approach
Wrote 10 SQL queries to slice the data by borrower features (FICO, DTI, employment) and loan terms, then built a 4-page Tableau dashboard for portfolio risk monitoring.
Key findings
Lending Club's high-risk grades (E–G) lost money even at 21–28% interest — defaults outpaced the pricing. 60-month loans defaulted at twice the rate of 36-month loans (32% vs 16%), for only 4.6 percentage points more interest.

AI Interview Coach
Capstone · 2025–2026
A bilingual interview-practice platform — the first of its kind for the Mongolian market.
Problem
Mongolian job-seekers have very limited access to realistic, affordable interview practice. International tools are English-only and miss local interview style and cultural context.
Approach
Built a full-stack bilingual (Mongolian/English) web platform: voice-to-text via Whisper, semantic answer evaluation using Sentence Transformers + TF-IDF, and feedback generation with the Claude API. Curated a dataset of 489 real question–answer pairs from 18 Mongolian companies and engineered a 5-dimension scoring system covering content, structure, clarity, depth, and relevance.
Results
Statistically validated the automatic scoring engine against human expert judgment, achieving significant agreement (p < 0.05). First Mongolian-language interview-prep platform of its kind.

Ulaanbaatar PM2.5 Forecasting
ML Benchmark Study · 2025
The first local machine-learning benchmark for hourly PM2.5 prediction in one of the world's most polluted winter capitals.
Problem
Ulaanbaatar suffers from severe winter air pollution, yet no local benchmark existed for hourly PM2.5 forecasting. Without a baseline, it's impossible to know whether complex models actually help — or whether simpler approaches are enough.
Approach
Collected 21,112 hourly observations and engineered 39 features (calendar, weather, lag-based). Trained and compared four models (Ridge regression, Random Forest, HistGBM, and an LSTM) using a strict chronological holdout to prevent data leakage.
Results
The best model — Ridge regression — reached R² = 0.899 and MAE = 3.70 µg/m³, outperforming the LSTM baseline. Key insight: lag features mattered more than model complexity for this problem.

Deepfake Detection
Multi-Paradigm Comparative Study · 2025
Benchmarking five deepfake-detection approaches under realistic image degradation.
Problem
Most deepfake-detection papers report accuracy on clean, pristine images — but real-world content is compressed, blurred, and noisy. The clean-image champion isn't always the best in production.
Approach
Benchmarked five methods (Fourier-frequency analysis, CNN, Vision Transformer, foundation models) on 10,000 StyleGAN face images under realistic degradation (Gaussian blur, JPEG compression). Used McNemar's test for pairwise statistical comparison.
Results
ResNet50 was best on clean images (98.2%), but ViT-B/16 was far more robust under blur (78.6% vs 59.9% for ResNet50). The best clean-image model is not the best real-world model.
